Planned hosted flow

Hemsted integration, with issuer binding.

The planned flow presents a credential-verification screen tied to a short-lived challenge. Production challenge creation remains disabled until a trusted issuer is configured.

Step 01

Issuer enables the flow

A supported identity provider issues credentials with provenance OTRUST can verify.

Step 02

Challenge starts at Hemsted

Hemsted creates a short-lived challenge and sends the user to otrust.eu.

Step 03

Credential and callback verified

OTRUST verifies issuer binding before returning a signed token and preserved state.

01

Partner creates challenge

02

03

Callback returns to Hemsted

The callback remains registered, scoped, and state-bound. Partner branding does not change auth semantics.
